# Pax private provision: building blocks (Bausteine)

Sources: the 5 sample quotes in `material/offerten/`, pax.ch product pages (fetched 2026-10-31), `material/pax-angemessenheitspruefung.pdf`. Figures are from sample quotes or marketing pages and are indicative only.

Every Pax private provision product is a **bundle of building blocks** in a **wrapper**. The advisor's job is to pick blocks against Vorsorgelücken; the product name follows from the bundle.

```
Vorsorgefall / goal ──► Baustein(e) ──► Rahmen (wrapper) ──► Pax product (label)
   Tod                  Todesfallkapital    Säule 3a/3b         Todesfallversicherung
   Erwerbsunfähigkeit   EU-Rente, Prämienbefreiung  Laufzeit     EU-Versicherung, LifeStar, FondsStar, Pax 3a
   Erleben / Ziel       Sparteil (garantiert + Fonds)  Zahlung   Pax 3a, LifeStar, FondsStar
   Auszahlung           Auszahlungsetappen             ...       Auszahlungsplan
   Kind                 Kinder-Spar + Kinder-EU-Rente            Kinderversicherung
```

## A. Risk blocks (Risikobausteine)

| Block | What it pays | Configurable | Found in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Todesfallkapital** | Fixed lump sum to beneficiaries on death | Sum, term, 3a/3b; early payout if life expectancy <12 months; funeral costs addable; from about CHF 8.35/month | Todesfallversicherung, LifeStar, FondsStar (as death benefit), Kinderversicherung (adult) |
| **Erwerbsunfähigkeitsrente** | Annual pension on disability; from 25% disability, full pension from 66.6%, until contract end or retirement | Amount (guaranteed up to CHF 36k), waiting period (e.g. 24 months), **exclude mental illness** (cheaper), **exclude accident**, entry tariff up to age 28 | EU-Versicherung, LifeStar, FondsStar, Kinderversicherung |
| **Prämienbefreiung bei EU** | Pax keeps paying the premiums when the customer can't work (savings goal still reached) | Waiting period (e.g. 3 months) | All savings products, EU-Versicherung, Pax 3a (core feature, e.g. CHF 8.90/month in the example) |
| **Kapitaloption «Hilfeleistungen»** | +CHF 10,000/year for 5 years on full disability, on top of the pension | On/off | EU-Versicherung |
| **Krisencoach** | Up to CHF 2,500 of coaching and organisational help after 90 days of disability | Included | EU riders |
| **Prämienbefreiung bei Familienzuwachs** | Pax pays the cost and risk premium for 4 months when a child is born | On/off | FondsStar |
| **Soforthilfe (Pax Care)** | CHF 2,000 on death plus up to 2 hours of personal support; free, ages 18–64, one beneficiary | Beneficiary (changeable in the portal) | Pax Care |
| **Kinder-EU-Rente** | Disability pension for the child (e.g. CHF 144/year for up to CHF 24k/year); passed to the child at 30 **without a health check** | Amount | Kinderversicherung |

## B. Saving blocks (Sparbausteine)

| Block | Description | Configurable |
|---|---|---|
| **Garantierter Anlageteil** | Classic investment with guaranteed interest (e.g. 0.35% on periodic premiums, 0.05% on single premiums) plus surplus | Share of premium: LifeStar variable; Pax 3a 90/70/50%; Kinderversicherung 10–95% |
| **Fondsanteil** | Fund-linked part; makes the product a *qualified life insurance* → **Angemessenheitsprüfung** required | Fund or strategy: Konservativ / Ausgewogen / Dynamisch (Pax Sustainable Portfolio funds, UBS MSCI World ETF, CH Small&Mid Cap) |
| **Automatische Fondsumschichtung** | Moves money into lower-risk investments every year towards the end | Start date |
| **Prämienform** | Periodic premium and/or single premium, top-ups, transfer of 3a money from elsewhere | Amount; monthly/quarterly/half-yearly/yearly payment |

## C. Payout and access blocks (Auszahlung & Zugriff)

| Block | Description |
|---|---|
| **Erlebensfallleistung** | Lump sum at contract end; can be postponed up to 5 years if still working |
| **Auszahlungsetappen** | Auszahlungsplan: capital paid out in stages (e.g. 3 stages over 20 years), guaranteed part + fund-linked part |
| **Privilegierter Vorbezug Wohneigentum** | Withdraw savings capital (3a) to buy a home for own use |
| **Verpfändung / Policendarlehen** | Pledge the policy for a mortgage, or borrow against it |
| **Rückkauf / Umwandlung** | Early cancellation (surrender value) or conversion to a premium-free policy |

## D. Wrapper and flexibility (Rahmen)

| Parameter | Options |
|---|---|
| **Vorsorgeart** | **Säule 3a** (tax-deductible up to CHF 7,258/year; beneficiaries set by law) or **Säule 3b** (free beneficiaries, simple online purchase) |
| **Säulenwechsel** | Switch 3a ↔ 3b during the contract (LifeStar, FondsStar) |
| **Laufzeit / Endalter** | Term up to retirement; LifeStar up to end age 70 |
| **Begünstigung** | Beneficiary order (3a by law; 3b free) |
| **Prämiendepot** | Prepay premiums into a deposit at a preferential rate |
| **Risikozwischenversicherung** | Pause saving and keep only risk cover (baby break, time abroad, further education) |
| **Ausbaugarantie** | **Increase the EU-Rente without a health check:** by up to CHF 12k on **marriage, divorce, buying a home, pay rise, end of child pension**; proportional to pay rises up to CHF 36k until age 30/31 |

## E. Pricing inputs (Tarifierung)

Age, gender, **smoker**, **Berufsgruppe** (job risk class from the job), health questions (Antrag), insured amounts, term, payment mode.

## Products as bundles

| Product | Blocks |
|---|---|
| **Pax Care** | Soforthilfe (free) |
| **Pax Todesfallversicherung** | Todesfallkapital (+ wrapper 3a/3b) |
| **Pax Erwerbsunfähigkeitsversicherung** | EU-Rente + Prämienbefreiung + Krisencoach (+ options: no mental illness, no accident, Kapitaloption, Ausbaugarantie, entry tariff ≤28) |
| **Pax 3a** | Sparteil (Sicherheit 90/10, Ausgewogen 70/30, Chance 50/50) + Prämienbefreiung bei EU; 100% digital, from CHF 50/month; 3a only |
| **Pax LifeStar** | Sparteil (variable guarantee split) + optional Todesfallkapital, EU-Rente, Prämienbefreiung, Krisencoach; Säulenwechsel, end age 70, Ausbaugarantie, Policendarlehen, Vorbezug |
| **Pax FondsStar** | 100% Fondsanteil + same risk riders + Prämienbefreiung bei Familienzuwachs; free fund switches, Vorbezug, Säulenwechsel |
| **Pax Kinderversicherung** | Kinder-Spar (10–95% guarantee) + Kinder-EU-Rente + co-insured adult + Prämienbefreiung on death or disability of the policyholder |
| **Pax Auszahlungsplan** | Single investment → Auszahlungsetappen (guaranteed + fund-linked), 3b |

## Mapping to our life story (age 26 → family)

| Life stage | Typical blocks | Hook |
|---|---|---|
| 26, single, first job | Soforthilfe (Pax Care), Sparteil 3a + Prämienbefreiung (Pax 3a), small EU-Rente with **entry tariff ≤28** | Tax saving up to about CHF 1,700/year; "free" first step |
| Moving in together (Adressänderung) | Todesfallkapital for the partner (3b with the partner as beneficiary), **Ausbaugarantie** top-up of the EU-Rente | No health check needed for the top-up after the life event |
| Buying a home | Vorbezug or Verpfändung, Todesfallkapital to secure the mortgage, Ausbaugarantie | Mortgage still affordable if one income is lost |
| Child | Prämienbefreiung bei Familienzuwachs (FondsStar), Kinderversicherung, higher Todesfallkapital | 4 months of premium relief, child's disability cover for life |
| Before retirement | Fondsumschichtung, Auszahlungsplan, staggered 3a withdrawals | Tax-optimised payout |
