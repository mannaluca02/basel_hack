		25 heading Problem Statement, Value: 2
			26 text Problem Statement
		27 text Customers in Switzerland lack a digital, self-service advisory channel for life insurance. Pax — an established Swiss life insurer with a fast-growing product portfolio — offers advice exclusively through traditional human advisors. Insurance is complex, and customers often do not know which risks they should insure, how much they need to provide for, which products fit their situation, or what the difference between the various solutions is. At the same time, AI is changing how people search for information, make decisions, and interact with companies. Pax wants to know what the insurance advisor of the future can look like. The challenge is therefore: build the future of insurance advice. Develop a working prototype of an AI Insurance Advisor that guides a customer through their personal pension situation and derives comprehensible recommendations for suitable Pax products. We are not looking for an ordinary chatbot. We are looking for an idea that shows us what a digital pension advisor would look like if it were really good.

			61 text Legal Framework
		62 text Customer, financial, and health data must be processed transparently with advance notice, and never sent to external AI models or cloud services uncontrolled. Every AI recommendation must be explainable, documented, and auditable on the basis of traceable criteria.
